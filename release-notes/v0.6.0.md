The driver and its tools now live in `/usr/local/mavergreen/trackpad2`, and their state in
`/usr/local/mavergreen/var/trackpad2`. `voodooinputmavericks-run` is on the path of every new login
shell. The Trackpad pane's SIMBL plugin is installed directly, and `sudo mavergreen uninstall trackpad2`
removes everything the package installed. Restart after installing, as before: the new driver loads
at the next boot.
